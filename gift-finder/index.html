Gifting Methodology & Guidelines

Frequently Asked Questions

How our editorial team approaches curation, budget matching, and gift psychology.

The Gift Finder uses an in-browser weighted scoring engine across our independently tested catalog. It cross-references recipient relationships, age ranges, strict price ceilings, passions (such as history, books, or science), occasions, and desired gift personality (e.g. heirloom or practical) without using external AI APIs or tracking personal data.

Our engine strictly filters candidate products against your chosen price tier before ranking. When you select Under $25, $25–$50, or $50–$100, items priced higher than the ceiling are excluded from your primary recommendations so you never see out-of-budget suggestions.

For recipients who readily purchase their own everyday necessities, we recommend narrative objects: single-harvest extra virgin olive oils in hand-painted ceramics, laboratory-grade optical prisms that refract natural sunlight, or bespoke desk ephemera crafted from solid brass and archival rag paper.

When shopping for someone with deep domain expertise (e.g., an archivist, chef, or astronomer), avoid guessing their core technical tools. Instead, choose items that celebrate the culture, history, or tactile ceremony of their craft—such as museum-grade cartography prints, unlacquered brass book weights, or facsimile primary sources.
